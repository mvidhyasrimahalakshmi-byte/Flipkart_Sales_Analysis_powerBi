# Flipkart Sales Analysis – Business Requirements

## 1. Business Requirement

The business requires an interactive Power BI dashboard to monitor retail sales performance, profitability, order volume, quantity sold, product performance, customer segments, payment behavior, gender distribution, and sales-channel performance.

## 2. KPI Requirements

- Total Orders
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Quantity
- Profit Margin %
- Total Customers

## 3. Visualization Requirements

- Monthly Sales and Profit – Line Chart
- Sales by Category – Donut Chart
- Customer Segment – Column Chart
- Payment Method – Donut/Pie Chart
- Top 10 Products by Sales – Bar Chart
- Sales by Gender – Donut Chart
- Sales Channel – Interactive analysis/filter

## 4. Filter Requirements

Users should be able to filter the dashboard using:

- Year
- Month
- Category
- Gender
- Customer Segment
- Payment Mode
- Sales Channel

## 5. Business Questions

- What are total sales and profit?
- How many orders and units were sold?
- How do sales and profit change month by month?
- Which categories generate the highest sales?
- Which products are the top performers?
- Which customer segments contribute to sales?
- Which payment methods are used?
- How are sales distributed by gender?
- How does sales performance vary by sales channel?
- How do KPIs and visualizations change when filters are applied?

## 6. Expected Outcome

The completed dashboard should provide a centralized interactive view of retail sales performance and enable stakeholders to monitor KPIs, analyze trends, compare products and categories, understand customer behavior, and evaluate payment and sales-channel performance.
